        About

        Why it exists

        Every winter, smog settles over Indian cities and families are told to “stay safe” without being told how. AirSense answers with specifics: which hour to open the windows, whether the school run can wait, what a metro ride is worth, and what a purifier changes in your own home.

        It was built for the Urban Air Quality Assistant challenge: a GenAI assistant that suggests low-emission routines, indoor air quality hacks and sustainable alternatives, tailored to location and lifestyle, and that clearly explains the direct impact of each choice.

        How the numbers work

        Air and weather data come from Open-Meteo and refresh every five minutes. Impact figures (CO₂, PM2.5 exposure, cigarette equivalents) are computed by formula on the server from published averages, so they are reproducible. The language model only explains them. The assumptions are listed in the Impact lab under “How we calculate”.

        The clearer skyline when many households act together is an illustration of collective effect, not a forecast.

        What it cannot do

        It is not medical advice and not a regulatory monitor. Modelled data can differ from a sensor on your street. If anyone at home has symptoms that persist, see a doctor.

        Accessibility and privacy

        Keyboard navigable, screen reader announcements for live changes, reduced motion support, high contrast text, and English and Tamil. Your profile stays in your browser. Only a place and lifestyle choices are sent to the server, and API keys never leave it.
